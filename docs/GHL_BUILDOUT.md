## 2. MAGIC LANTERN — teardown series (trigger: applied/opted-in but not booked after 2 days)
Day 2 / 5 / 9. Value first; each opens a loop the next one closes.

**Email 1 — subject: `the first thing we check on any account`**
> {{contact.first_name}},
>
> When we audit a business, the first thing we open isn't the ads — it's what happens in the five minutes after someone fills out a form.
>
> Industry data has said the same thing for fifteen years: contact a lead in 5 minutes and you're ~8× more likely to reach them than at 30 minutes. Most businesses answer the next morning. Their "ad problem" is usually a follow-up problem wearing an ad problem's clothes.
>
> Test your own business right now: fill out your own contact form, start a timer, see what happens. That number is your real marketing.
>
> Next: the homepage mistake that quietly filters out 97% of your buyers.
>
> — 8th & Exchange Media

**Email 2 — subject: `the 3% homepage`**
> {{contact.first_name}},
>
> At any moment, roughly 3% of your market is ready to buy today. The other 97% are curious, comparing, or don't yet know they have the problem you solve.
>
> Now look at your homepage. If every button says some version of "buy now / book now / get a quote," you built the whole site for the 3% — and handed the 97% to whoever educates them first.
>
> The fix costs almost nothing: one genuinely useful thing a visitor can take in exchange for an email, and three follow-ups that help before they ask for anything. That's it. That's the machine.
>
> Last one from me Friday: the number that decides how much you can afford to spend on ads (most owners guess it; it's calculable).
>
> — 8th & Exchange Media

**Email 3 — subject: `the only ad budget math that works`**
> {{contact.first_name}},
>
> Skip percentages-of-revenue. Two questions:
>
> 1) What's a customer worth over their whole relationship with you? (LTV)
> 2) What will you pay to acquire one? (Most owners can afford 3× what they think — the winner in any market is usually whoever can *spend the most per customer*, not whoever's ads are cleverest.)
>
> That math is page one of every Growth Map we write. If you want yours calculated with your numbers: [CALENDAR LINK] — 30 minutes, and the written map is yours whether or not we ever work together.
>
> — 8th & Exchange Media
